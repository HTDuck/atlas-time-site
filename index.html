How it works

The map is the clock.

Map daylight across the world

As Earth rotates, sunlight sweeps across its curved surface. The day–night terminator traces where day meets night.

Explore time and geography

One finger swipe moves through time. Pinch to zoom then drag with two fingers to explore the map.

Choose any date

Tap Reference Time to choose a date and time.

Return to Live

Tap Return to Live to resume the current time.

Use landscape

Rotate for a wider map with the city list at the side. Show or hide it with the city-list control.

Manage city list

Tap Add City to search for a city. Swipe left to delete, tap to focus its beacon, or long-press and drag to reorder.
