Support

Most of Atlas Time is designed to be understood by moving through the map.

Quick answers

How do I return to the current time?

Tap Return to Live. The map, Reference Time, UTC, and every selected city return to now together.

How do I explore another date?

Tap Reference Time at the top of the app, choose a date and time, then select View This Time.

How do I add, remove, or reorder cities?

Use Add City to search the offline catalog. Swipe a city card left to delete it. To reorder, long-press until the card lifts and drag it to the highlighted insertion line.

Does Atlas Time need an internet connection?

No. Core maps, time-zone calculations, city search, daylight, sunrise, and sunset behavior are bundled for offline use.

Does Atlas Time need my location?

No. Atlas Time uses your device's configured time zone for Reference Time and lets you choose cities manually.

Where are purchases and refunds handled?

Atlas Time is purchased through the App Store. Apple manages billing, purchase history, and refund requests.
